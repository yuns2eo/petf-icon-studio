# JELLY ICON — CHARACTER GUIDE (몽실이 & 방실이)

## Purpose

Use this file whenever a request names 몽실이 (Mongsil) or 방실이 (Bangsil), alone or inside a combination with other icons.

Read `style/JELLY_Style_Lock.md` first. The style lock still controls material, lighting, background, and composition. This file fixes the two characters' identity; **their colors are fixed and never follow the cat / dog / common version colors.**

The reference images are the visual source of truth. Always attach **both Front + 4View** of each character that appears, including when editing a previous result (see **Reference attachment** in the style lock):

- `references/characters/Mongsil_Front.png` / `Mongsil_4View.png`
- `references/characters/Bangsil_Front.png` / `Bangsil_4View.png`

---

## Shared identity (both characters)

- a cat **head only** — no body, no paws, no tail
- a wide, soft mochi-like head shape: rounded top, slightly flattened wide bottom
- two rounded triangular ears with pink inner ears
- glossy jelly material, same as the icon set
- two small round glossy black eyes, set wide apart
- soft pink oval blush on both cheeks
- two thin dark-brown whiskers on each side, gently curved
- calm, sweet, closed-mouth smile; no teeth, no tongue, no eyebrows

## 몽실이 (Mongsil)

- warm ivory / cream body color
- one caramel-brown patch covering the **right ear and the upper-right forehead** (viewer's right)
- a small white puffy muzzle made of two round cheek bumps under the nose
- small dark-brown rounded triangle nose sitting on top of the muzzle
- small W-shaped mouth under the muzzle

Group: placed with the **cat** icons when sorting.

## 방실이 (Bangsil)

- milky pure white body color, no patches anywhere
- no raised muzzle; the face is flat and smooth
- small dark-brown rounded triangle nose
- a wider W-shaped smile directly under the nose

Group: placed with the **dog** icons when sorting.

---

## What may freely change

- the background (black default, white on request)
- the camera angle, using the matching 4-view reference
- being placed inside a combination (see the style lock's combination rule) — e.g. peeking out of a basket, sitting in a bowl

## Non-negotiable rules

Never:

- add a body, paws, clothes, or accessories unless the request explicitly asks for them
- swap the patch side, add more patches, or give 방실이 a patch
- recolor the characters with the version pink or teal
- change the eye size, eye spacing, nose, muzzle, mouth, blush, or whiskers
- turn them into a realistic cat, a flat illustration, or a different mascot

Element names: `몽실이`, `방실이`
